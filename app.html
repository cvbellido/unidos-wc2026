How the World Cup bracket works

▼
How many teams are in the 2026 World Cup?
48 teams compete across 12 groups. It's the first World Cup with this expanded format — more teams, more matches, more chaos.
How does the Group Stage work?
Each group has 4 teams playing round-robin (3 matches per team). The top 2 from each group advance automatically. The 4 best 3rd-place finishers from each pod (8 total) also advance as wild cards.
What's the "Best 8 Third-Place" rule?
12 teams finish 3rd in their group. Only the 8 best (ranked by points, then goal difference, then goals scored) advance to the Round of 32. That's why you rank them TP1–TP8 — your ranking determines their bracket seeding.
What is the Round of 32?
The Round of 32 is the first knockout round — 32 teams, 16 matches. Win and you advance; lose and you're out. The 32 entrants are: 12 group winners, 12 runners-up, and the 8 best third-place teams.
How do the knockout rounds progress?
After R32 comes the Round of 16 (16 teams), then Quarterfinals (8), Semifinals (4), and the Final (2). Each round eliminates half the field until one champion is crowned.
Is there a 3rd place match?
Yes — the two teams that lose in the Semifinals play a 3rd place match. This doesn't affect bracket scoring.

How scoring works

You earn points whenever a team you picked advances correctly. Later rounds are worth more.

Group stage finishers (1st & 2nd per group)1 pt each
Round of 32 winners2 pts each
Round of 16 winners4 pts each
Quarterfinal winners8 pts each
Semifinal winners16 pts each
Champion32 pts
Maximum possible184 pts
